Known Bugs — Most Serious First

#BugFileSeverity
1 Meta realtime WS handshake not confirmed — "Malformed handshake request" or silent close. Live transcription may still fail. server/routes/transcribe.js Critical
2 Parsed sale result is never saved to Supabase. The whole point of the app (bookkeeping) is not connected to the database. MicTest.jsx → no DB write Critical
3 WebSocket stays open after stop is sent if Meta doesn't reply in time — no timeout, no forced close. Can leak connections. server/routes/transcribe.js High
4 If the user presses Stop before the WS is OPEN (still CONNECTING), the "stop" text frame is silently dropped and the upstream session is never closed. useVoiceRecorder.js line ~90 High
5 No RLS policy on transaction_items allows insert — only select is covered by the policy as written. supabase/schema.sql High
